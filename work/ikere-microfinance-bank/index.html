Project overview

A professional web platform presenting the bank’s services and giving customers a clear digital route to information.

The challenge

A financial institution’s public website must communicate services and trust clearly across phones and desktop devices.

The solution

I delivered a structured public platform focused on accessible service information, institutional credibility and straightforward customer navigation.

Key features

  • Financial-service information architecture
  • Responsive public pages
  • Clear customer pathways
  • Maintainable content structure

Technical approach

The product uses Laravel · PHP · Blade in a maintainable web architecture. The interface is responsive and the public information hierarchy is designed around the organisation’s users and content.

Outcome

The delivered platform gives the organisation a working digital product for its stated purpose. No unsupported traffic, revenue or conversion claims are made.
