Project overview

An EdTech product focused on structured, affordable and locally relevant coding education for young Africans.

The challenge

Learning to code is easier when the learning path, context and delivery model reflect the people the product serves.

The solution

I developed BootKode as a focused education platform for structured technical learning with a locally relevant product direction.

Key features

  • Structured learning pathways
  • Education-focused product architecture
  • Clear programme presentation
  • Responsive learner experience

Technical approach

The product uses Laravel · PHP · Blade in a maintainable web architecture. The interface is responsive and the public information hierarchy is designed around the organisation’s users and content.

Outcome

The delivered platform gives the organisation a working digital product for its stated purpose. No unsupported traffic, revenue or conversion claims are made.
