Project overview

A broadcasting platform bringing live radio, programmes, presenters, news and audience touchpoints into one digital home.

The challenge

A radio organisation needs more than a brochure website. Its digital platform must present changing programmes and editorial content clearly while giving listeners direct paths to live radio and the station.

The solution

I built a Laravel-based media platform structured around broadcasting content, public discovery and manageable editorial workflows.

Key features

  • Live radio and broadcast-focused public experience
  • Programme, presenter and editorial content areas
  • Audience contact and organisational information
  • Administrative workflows for managing site content

Technical approach

The product uses Laravel · PHP · Livewire in a maintainable web architecture. The interface is responsive and the public information hierarchy is designed around the organisation’s users and content.

Outcome

The delivered platform gives the organisation a working digital product for its stated purpose. No unsupported traffic, revenue or conversion claims are made.
