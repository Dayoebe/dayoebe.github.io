Project overview

A public information and coordination platform connecting community health outreach with role-aware digital workspaces.

The challenge

The initiative needed a clear public presence alongside protected workflows for the different people involved in outreach and service coordination.

The solution

I developed a Laravel and Livewire platform that combines public service information with role-aware areas for residents, volunteers, healthcare professionals, partners, representatives, staff and administrators.

Key features

  • Public outreach and service information
  • Role-aware dashboards and access boundaries
  • Volunteer and healthcare-professional pathways
  • Partner, representative, staff and admin workspaces

Technical approach

The product uses Laravel · Livewire · PHP in a maintainable web architecture. The interface is responsive and the public information hierarchy is designed around the organisation’s users and content.

Outcome

The delivered platform gives the organisation a working digital product for its stated purpose. No unsupported traffic, revenue or conversion claims are made.
