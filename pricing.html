Pricing questions

Before requesting a quote.

Are these fixed project prices?

No. They are verified starting prices. Your final quote reflects the agreed requirements, complexity and delivery plan.

How accurate are converted currency prices?

They are estimates calculated from the base NGN rates using current or recently saved exchange rates. The agreed quote confirms the final amount and payment currency.

Can a project be delivered in phases?

Yes, where the requirements can be divided into useful stages. Phasing is discussed during scope planning.

What happens after the free discussion?

If the project is a good fit, I prepare the appropriate scope and quote. Work begins after agreement and the 25% kickoff payment.
