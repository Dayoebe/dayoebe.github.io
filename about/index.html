About Adedayo

I build systems for people doing real work.

Full-stack developer, Industrial Mathematics graduate, educator and founder—based in Akure, Nigeria and building for the world.

My perspective

I specialise in Laravel and PHP, with a focus on EdTech, business platforms and custom web applications. I care about dependable backend systems, understandable interfaces and software that respects the day-to-day reality of its users.

I studied Industrial Mathematics at the Federal University of Technology, Akure. That analytical foundation sits alongside experience across software development, education, media and business systems.

Current direction

I am focused on reliable Laravel applications, education technology, operational tools and digital products for remote clients and product teams. I am available for project work and relevant full-stack Laravel roles.
