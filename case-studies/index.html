Media operations

Glow 99.1 FM

Challenge: The station needed a stronger public presence and a structured platform for content, programmes, presenters, schedules, audience discovery, and digital growth.

My role: I build and maintain the web platform and support the connection between publishing operations and public audience experiences.

Delivered:

  • Responsive public media experience
  • News and content publishing workflows
  • Show, schedule, and presenter discovery
  • Live-listening entry points and audience-focused navigation
  • Administrative and digital-growth foundations

Stack: Laravel, Livewire, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, MySQL.

Community health

Glow Health Outreach Initiative

Challenge: Residents needed clear public healthcare information while volunteers, clinicians, partners, representatives, staff, and administrators needed governed participation and coordination.

My role: I designed and built the Laravel and Livewire foundation spanning public communication, secure accounts, participant roles, administration, and delegated work.

Delivered:

  • Services, outreach, impact, volunteer, partner, and contact pages
  • Secure authentication and account-security workflows
  • Seven participation categories with tailored dashboards
  • Protected role-change approval and separate administrator privileges
  • Task delegation and email notification foundations
  • Responsive PWA-ready public experience

Honest scope: Several deeper operational modules remain roadmap items. The case study describes implemented foundations rather than claiming the entire roadmap is complete.

Stack: Laravel, Livewire, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, MySQL, PWA.
